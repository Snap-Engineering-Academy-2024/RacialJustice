Final Project for my group's Snap Academies Showcase presentation. 

Our team was tasked with developing a prototype for the Snapchat app that highlights an issue affecting minority communities. 
Our group was given racial justice, hence why that's the name of our repo.

We were provided with a barebones starter codebase that only displayed the basic Snapchat interface, including the default screen and navigation icons at the bottom of the app.
From there, our team designed, developed, and tested a prototype that expands on the original framework to showcase our concept and user experience ideas.
